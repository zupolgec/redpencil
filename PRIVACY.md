# redpencil privacy policy

redpencil is a tool you run on your own computer. The Chrome extension
collects no personal data and sends nothing to us or to anyone else.

- **What it reads.** On the sites you turn on with its icon, the extension
  loads the comment panel into the page. Nothing happens on any other site.
- **Where your comments go.** Comments, and the screenshots of the areas you
  draw, go only to the redpencil server running on your computer
  (127.0.0.1 or localhost). They are saved as files in your project folder.
- **What it stores in the browser.** The list of sites you turned on, so the
  panel comes back when you reopen them. Click the icon again to remove a
  site.
- **Screenshots.** They are taken only when you draw an area on a page that
  is on screen, and only of that tab. Chrome asks for access to all sites
  because that is the only way it allows screenshots of any site you choose.
- **No analytics, no tracking, no remote code.** The extension contacts no
  server other than the one on your computer.

The coding agent that works on your comments (Claude Code, Codex, opencode)
is a separate program you choose and run yourself; its own terms apply.

Questions: open an issue on the project's repository.
